SplitItUpNow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

SplitItUpNow ("the app", "we") is an expense-splitting app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices. Questions: masood.vaheed@1digitalstack.ai.

Information we collect

  • Account info — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Profile you set — a display name and optional profile photo.
  • Expense & group data — groups, members, expenses, amounts, categories, dates, settlements, and receipt photos you add.
  • People you add — names and (optionally) email addresses of people you choose to add to your list or invite.
  • Device push token — if you enable notifications, an Expo push token so we can notify you of group activity.

How we use it

  • To provide the core service: creating groups, splitting expenses, and calculating balances.
  • To sync your data across your devices and back it up.
  • To let people you invite join your groups, and to notify group members of new expenses, settle-ups, and joins.

How it's stored and shared

  • Your data is stored locally on your device and, when signed in, synced to our backend (Supabase).
  • Data in a group (expenses, member names, receipt photos, group photos) is visible to the other members of that group — that's the purpose of a shared group.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. There are no ads in the app.

Third-party services

  • Google — sign-in (authentication).
  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage, and serverless functions.
  • Resend — sending invitation emails you initiate.
  • Expo — push notification delivery.

Data retention & deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data (groups you own, expenses, contacts, profile, stored photos) from our backend. Data also lives locally on your device until you delete the app.

Children

SplitItUpNow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date reflects the latest version.
